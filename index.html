Questions, answered

A tool for making images feel intentional.

Everything here describes what the editor does today. The best way to learn the motion system is to start with one small collection.

Does Images of Yours use my browser?

Yes. The editor renders the media you choose in the browser, including the preview and available browser export capabilities.

What can I make with it?

Create motion showcases from images and video assets. Templates give the composition its movement; scenes, transitions, camera controls, and keyframes shape the story.

Can I make a vertical social video?

Yes. Select the 9:16 frame preset, choose a template that suits portrait composition, then export at 1080p when you are ready.

Can I save a work in progress?

Yes. Projects can be saved as an .ioy file and opened again in the editor. Images are embedded; larger video files may need to be added again.
